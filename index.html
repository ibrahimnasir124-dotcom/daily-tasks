Settings & backup

Sync between your devices

Google client ID
Your tasks and daily log are kept in a private, hidden file in your own Google Drive that only this app can see. Sign in on each device (phone and PC) with the same Google account. Changes sync within seconds while the app is open.
Sync works in the web version of this app: ibrahimnasir124-dotcom.github.io/daily-tasks. Move your tasks there with Save a backup file and Restore from a file.

Automatic backup

Every change is saved to DailyTasks-backup.json in the folder you choose, plus one copy per day (the last 30 days are kept).
To back up to Google Drive: install Google Drive for desktop, then pick a folder inside My Drive (usually G:\My Drive). Drive syncs it to the cloud for you.
Works in Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. If the browser asks, choose Allow on every visit so backups keep running after a restart.

Reminders

(clear it for none)

Manual backup

On your phone: tap Restore from a file and pick DailyTasks-backup.json from Google Drive to load your PC's tasks. Tap Save a backup file and choose Drive to send your changes back.

Danger zone

Send tasks to Google Calendar

  1. Find DailyTasks.ics in your Downloads.
  2. Open Google Calendar's Import page on this PC, signed in to the same Google account as your Pixel.
  3. Choose the file, pick a calendar, and click Import.
Your Pixel's Calendar app gets the events within a minute and alerts you at each task's reminder time.
The calendar doesn't update itself. After adding or changing tasks, send them again: delete the old Daily Tasks calendar in Google Calendar settings first, create it again, and import, so nothing is doubled. Ticking a task off here doesn't remove it from the calendar.
If the phone doesn't alert for imported events, open the Daily Tasks calendar's settings in Google Calendar and set its default notification (for example "0 minutes before").
